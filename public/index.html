Meeting notes

          
Say "Hey Tulai, start meeting notes". Tulai listens through the microphone, writes the transcript and makes the summary on this Mac, so it uses no tokens. Tell the others you are transcribing.

          

          
What Tulai has learned about you

          
Learned on this Mac only: how often you ask, what you repeat, and when something frustrates you, so Tulai can adapt. It never leaves your computer.

          

          
Calendars (read in the background)

          
Tulai Bot opens your Outlook, Google or Teams calendar out of sight every few minutes, reads your meetings, and reminds you 5 minutes before each one. Turn one on, press Sign in, sign in yourself in the window that opens (Tulai never types your password), then close it. If it ever needs you to sign in again, it will tell you.
