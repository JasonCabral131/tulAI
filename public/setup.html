Getting Tulai ready

Downloading the AI that runs on your Mac.

Fine-tuning takes about 15 to 40 minutes. Keep the Mac plugged in; you can use Tulai meanwhile. Each skill is tested before it is used, and your current model stays if a test fails.

Skipping is fine: you can fine-tune later in Settings, Train your local bot AI, Skill packs.

Everything stays on this Mac. Tulai never sends your voice or files anywhere. You only need to do this once.
